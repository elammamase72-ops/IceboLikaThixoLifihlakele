Our Church

Our vision, mission, motto and the values that guide us.

Our Vision

We see Icebo Lika Thixo Lifihlakele as a dynamic, Holy Spirit-filled, multicultural church, numbering in the thousands, impacting our city, our nation and our world through leadership development and church planting.

Our Mission

To reach and connect the unconnected to Christ and together pursue full devotion to Him while empowering and impacting our communities.

Our Motto

Home... where Christ is at the centre of it all... Building families, impacting communities and changing the world... One soul at a time.
